This Cookie Policy explains how GlowUp Beauty ("we," "our," or "us") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website. It explains what these technologies are and why we use them, as well as your rights to control our use of them.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to website owners about how users interact with their sites.

Types of Cookies:

  • Session Cookies: Temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser
  • Persistent Cookies: Remain on your device until they expire or are manually deleted
  • First-Party Cookies: Set by our website directly
  • Third-Party Cookies: Set by external services we use (like Google Analytics and AdSense)

2. How We Use Cookies

Essential Cookies (Always Active)

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly and cannot be disabled:

  • Website Functionality: Remember your preferences like theme settings
  • Security: Protect against security threats and ensure safe browsing
  • Performance: Basic functionality like navigation and page loading

Analytics Cookies

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website:

  • Purpose: Analyze website traffic, user behavior, and popular content
  • Data Collected: Page views, session duration, bounce rate, traffic sources
  • Retention: Up to 26 months
  • Opt-out: Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on

Advertising Cookies

We participate in Google AdSense to display relevant advertisements:

  • Purpose: Show personalized ads based on your interests and browsing history
  • Data Collected: Ad interactions, preferences, demographic information (anonymous)
  • Personalization: Ads are tailored to your interests across websites
  • Opt-out: Digital Advertising Alliance Opt-out

Social Media Cookies

When you share our content on social media platforms, they may set cookies:

  • Platforms: Pinterest, Instagram, Facebook, Twitter
  • Purpose: Enable sharing functionality and track social engagement
  • Control: Managed by each social media platform's privacy settings

4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Browser Settings

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies and Website Data
  • Edge: Settings > Site Permissions > Cookies and Site Data

Opt-Out Links

  • Google Analytics Opt-out
  • Google Ad Personalization
  • Digital Advertising Alliance Opt-out
  • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance

Impact of Disabling Cookies

Disabling certain cookies may affect your experience:

  • Essential Cookies: Website may not function properly
  • Analytics Cookies: We can't improve user experience based on usage data
  • Advertising Cookies: Ads may be less relevant to your interests
